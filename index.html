How it works

Three steps. About two minutes.

  1. Choose a templateBrowse nine layouts, from a formal classic to a bold banner and a group project table.
  2. Fill in your detailsFields change with the document type. Anything you leave empty disappears from the page.
  3. Download and submitExport as a sharp A4 PDF or a JPG image, or print straight from the browser.

Privacy

Free. No login. Private by design.

Everything runs in your browser. Your name, roll and logos are never uploaded. Your last draft is saved on this device so it is ready next time, and you can clear it any time from the Studio.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no accounts, limits or watermarks on the cover page.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser on this device. Use Clear saved data in the Studio to remove it.

Which formats can I download?

A4 PDF and JPG. You can also print directly. Letter size, landscape and custom sizes are available too.

Can I write in Bangla?

Yes. Every field accepts Bangla or mixed text, and there are several Bangla fonts to choose from.

Can I use my own logo?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG in the Logos section and preview it before using it.

Is this an official college website?

No. It is a student tool made for BBAGC students. College names and logos belong to their owners.
